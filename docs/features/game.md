# Statistics and the household game

## Statistics

Articles and videos read this week and over 30 days, the reading time, the most read topics and sources, per profile.

## The household game

Reading earns points, by the rules in `gamification.yml`. Only articles and videos a feed listed earn them, with the
topic and publication time the server knows. A profile counts at most 120 articles, 120 videos, 40 alerts, 40 shares
and 40 reactions a day, for its points as for its badges, so that no one can farm them.

Points raise a profile's level, shown as the ring around its avatar, and earn badges in bronze, silver and gold. Tapping
an avatar opens that profile's sheet: its grade, its points and its badges. On its own sheet, a profile sees every
badge, those still to earn dimmed with their progress, and picks an earned one to wear on its avatar. On a tablet or a
phone, the sheet also has **Switch profile**.

_Statistics_ opens on the household's week: the weekly challenge, articles to read together by Sunday, and the ranking
by this week's points. The admin turns the game off in _Settings › Household_: nothing of it shows then, but points keep
counting.
