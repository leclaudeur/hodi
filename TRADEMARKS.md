# Name and logo

Hodi's code is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE): you may use, change and share it, as that licence says.
The licence covers the code, but not the name or the logo. This page says how you may use them.

> [!NOTE]
> The name “Hodi” and the Hodi logo belong to Le Claudeur, the project's author. The logo is the “hod!” wordmark and
> its icons, kept in `web/public/` and `docs/assets/`.

## What you may do without asking

- **Say where your work comes from**: “a fork of Hodi”, “built on Hodi”, “compatible with Hodi”.
- **Run an unmodified copy** under its own name, for yourself, your household or your organisation.
- **Talk about the project** with its name and logo: articles, talks, videos, links back to this repository.

## What needs another name

- **A modified version for other people**: give it its own name and logo. “Based on Hodi” is welcome.
- **A product or a paid service**: neither the name nor the logo without written permission.
- **Look-alike names**: no “Hodi Pro”, “Hodi Cloud”… and no domains built on the name.

This follows section 7 of the AGPL-3.0, which lets an author decline rights over trade names, and require that changed
versions be marked as different from the original. For any other use, open an issue to ask.
