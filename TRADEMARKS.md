# Name and logo

The code of Hodi is free software under the [GNU AGPL-3.0](LICENSE): you may use, change and share it, as that licence
says. The licence covers the code, not the name or the logo. The name “Hodi” and the Hodi logo (the “hod!” wordmark and
its icons in `web/public/` and `docs/assets/`) belong to Le Claudeur, the project’s author. This note says how you
may use them.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your work uses, is based on or is compatible with Hodi, for example “a fork of Hodi” or “built on Hodi”.
- Run an unmodified copy of Hodi under its own name, for yourself, your household or your organisation.
- Use the name and the logo to talk about the project: articles, talks, videos, links back to this repository.

## What needs another name

- A modified version you share or run for other people: give it its own name and its own logo, so that nobody takes it
  for the original. Saying where it comes from (“based on Hodi”) is welcome.
- A product or a paid service: do not call it Hodi, and do not use the logo, without written permission.
- Names that could be mistaken for Hodi (“Hodi Pro”, “Hodi Cloud”…) or domains built on the name.

This follows section 7 of the AGPL-3.0, which lets an author decline rights over trade names and require that changed
versions be marked as different from the original. For any other use, open an issue to ask.
